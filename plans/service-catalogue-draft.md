# Service catalogue draft (PROVISIONAL)

> **Status: provisional draft for owner review. Not applied to any service record.**
> Every description below is **inferred from the service name only**. These are not established business offerings, commitments or quotes.
> No prices, timelines or guarantees are stated, and none should be added unless the owner supplies them.
> Finding codes from website audits are **observations**, not estimates of work size. An audit is a single-page, read-only observation and cannot by itself justify a project-sized service.

Fields per service: purpose, typical deliverables, suited when, exclusions and boundaries, engagement type, and the additional evidence needed before recommending it.

## Coverage check for small technical fixes

Only **Ongoing Maintenance & Technical Support** could plausibly cover small fixes, and it is **recurring** by name. No other existing service covers one-off fixes of audit-observed issues. A separate one-off **Technical Remediation** offering is proposed as a candidate (drafted at the end). It is **not added** and needs owner approval.

## 1. AI Integration & AI-Powered Applications
- **Engagement:** project (one-off build; support could follow separately).
- **Purpose:** add AI features to an existing or new application.
- **Typical deliverables:** use-case definition, integration with an AI provider, prompt and workflow design, evaluation and safeguards, handover notes.
- **Suited when:** repetitive text, data or search tasks could be assisted, or a team wants an assistant inside a product.
- **Exclusions:** training models from scratch; accuracy guarantees; third-party usage costs.
- **Evidence needed:** a concrete workflow and data source from a conversation. Website audit findings never support this service.

## 2. API Development & Third-Party Integrations
- **Engagement:** project.
- **Purpose:** build APIs and connect business systems.
- **Typical deliverables:** API design and documentation, endpoints, authentication, named third-party integrations, tests.
- **Suited when:** systems do not exchange data and work is re-keyed manually.
- **Exclusions:** third-party licence fees and third-party changes; user interfaces unless agreed.
- **Evidence needed:** an inventory of the systems involved. Public website evidence is rarely enough.

## 3. Booking & Reservation Systems
- **Engagement:** project.
- **Purpose:** let customers book appointments, rooms or tables online.
- **Typical deliverables:** booking flow, availability rules, confirmations, admin management, calendar sync.
- **Suited when:** bookings are handled by phone or email.
- **Exclusions:** payment processing (see Payment Gateway Integration) unless combined; third-party channel fees.
- **Evidence needed:** confirmation that bookings are taken and currently handled manually. The current audit does not collect this.

## 4. Business Process Automation
- **Engagement:** project (optional follow-on support).
- **Purpose:** automate repetitive internal workflows.
- **Typical deliverables:** process mapping, automated workflows, integrations, documentation.
- **Suited when:** staff repeat manual, rule-based tasks.
- **Exclusions:** organisation-wide change management; process redesign beyond the agreed workflows.
- **Evidence needed:** discussion of the actual processes.

## 5. CRM & ERP Systems
- **Engagement:** project, usually with recurring support.
- **Purpose:** implement or tailor a CRM or ERP.
- **Typical deliverables:** requirements, configuration or customisation, data migration, integrations, training.
- **Suited when:** customer or operational data is scattered across tools.
- **Exclusions:** software licence costs; data cleansing beyond the agreed scope.
- **Evidence needed:** current tools and data landscape from a conversation.

## 6. Custom E-Commerce Platforms & Online Stores
- **Engagement:** project.
- **Purpose:** build or replace an online store.
- **Typical deliverables:** store build, catalogue structure, checkout, payment hookup, admin, deployment setup.
- **Suited when:** a business sells products and has no store, or one that limits it.
- **Exclusions:** product photography and copy; marketing; stock-system integrations unless agreed.
- **Evidence needed:** that the business sells products, and that the current store is absent or inadequate. Header or metadata findings are not enough.

## 7. Custom Website Design & Development
- **Engagement:** project.
- **Purpose:** design and build a new website.
- **Typical deliverables:** design, build, content management, responsive layout, launch.
- **Suited when:** there is no website, or the existing one cannot meet the business goals.
- **Exclusions:** copywriting and photography; ongoing maintenance (see Ongoing Maintenance); hosting fees.
- **Evidence needed:** no site, or a site clearly inadequate for its purpose. A handful of low-severity page findings is not sufficient.

## 8. Customer Portals & Admin Dashboards
- **Engagement:** project.
- **Purpose:** give customers or staff authenticated self-service and reporting.
- **Typical deliverables:** login and roles, portal screens, dashboards, reports.
- **Suited when:** customers need self-service, or internal data lives in spreadsheets.
- **Exclusions:** building the underlying systems unless combined with the backend service.
- **Evidence needed:** needs established in a conversation.

## 9. Database Architecture & Backend Development
- **Engagement:** project.
- **Purpose:** design data models and backend services.
- **Typical deliverables:** schema design, backend services, migrations, data-layer hardening.
- **Suited when:** the data layer is slow or unreliable, or a new product needs a backend.
- **Exclusions:** front-end interfaces; data entry.
- **Evidence needed:** system knowledge or access from a conversation.

## 10. Full-Stack Web Application Development
- **Engagement:** project.
- **Purpose:** build a custom web application end to end.
- **Typical deliverables:** interface, API and database, authentication, deployment.
- **Suited when:** a bespoke web application is needed.
- **Exclusions:** ongoing operations; content creation.
- **Evidence needed:** defined requirements.

## 11. Mobile App Development
- **Engagement:** project.
- **Purpose:** build native or cross-platform mobile apps.
- **Typical deliverables:** iOS and Android app, store submission, backend connection.
- **Suited when:** a dedicated app is wanted for device features, offline use or an app-store presence.
- **Exclusions:** fixing a website's mobile layout (responsive web issues are not app work); store fees.
- **Evidence needed:** an explicit app requirement. Website mobile findings do not support this.

## 12. Ongoing Maintenance & Technical Support
- **Engagement:** recurring.
- **Purpose:** keep an existing site or application healthy and supported.
- **Typical deliverables:** updates, small fixes and changes, monitoring, support requests.
- **Suited when:** a site needs continued care.
- **Exclusions:** new feature development; redesign.
- **Evidence needed:** the owner's access to the site and the current setup. Whether a recurring arrangement is appropriate rather than a one-off fix is a decision for the owner.

## 13. Payment Gateway Integration
- **Engagement:** project (typically smaller, one-off).
- **Purpose:** accept online payments through a gateway.
- **Typical deliverables:** gateway integration, checkout flow, webhooks, test transactions, short operating notes.
- **Suited when:** a business takes payments manually or off-site.
- **Exclusions:** merchant account approval; compliance certification; gateway fees.
- **Evidence needed:** how the business currently takes payment.

## 14. SaaS Platform Development
- **Engagement:** project (large), possibly followed by support.
- **Purpose:** turn a service into a subscription software product.
- **Typical deliverables:** multi-customer application, onboarding, billing integration, admin tools.
- **Suited when:** a validated product concept needs building.
- **Exclusions:** marketing; customer support operations.
- **Evidence needed:** a defined product concept.

## 15. Website Redesign & Modernisation
- **Engagement:** project.
- **Purpose:** rework an existing website's design and platform.
- **Typical deliverables:** redesigned layout, modern build or migration, responsive behaviour, content migration.
- **Suited when:** the site as a whole is outdated or on an unsupported platform, or a rebrand is planned.
- **Exclusions:** custom application features; copywriting; individual small fixes (a separate, smaller scope).
- **Evidence needed:** site-wide evidence (multiple pages, overall design and technology age) plus the owner's goals. Single-page attribute, header or metadata findings are never sufficient on their own.

## Candidate (NOT ADDED): Technical Remediation (one-off)
- **Engagement:** one-off, fixed scope.
- **Purpose:** correct specific issues observed on an audited page.
- **Typical deliverables:** an itemised list of the agreed fixes, implementation, before and after verification.
- **Suited when:** an owner wants particular observed issues fixed without a redesign or a retainer.
- **Exclusions:** redesign; new features; ongoing maintenance; work that needs access the owner has not granted.
- **Evidence needed:** findings confirmed in context (audit output is an observation, not a diagnosis), and access to the hosting or content system.
- **Overlap:** partly overlaps Ongoing Maintenance & Technical Support, which is recurring. This candidate differs in being one-off and fixed in scope.
